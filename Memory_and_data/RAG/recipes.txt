Grandma's Secret Pancakes:
Ingredients: 2 cups flour, 2 eggs, 1.5 cups milk, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, pinch of salt.
Instructions: Mix dry ingredients. Whisk wet ingredients. Combine and cook on a hot griddle with oil ( no butter ). Serve with maple syrup.

Special Start-Your-Day Smoothie:
Ingredients: 1 banana, 1 cup spinach, 1/2 cup blueberries, 1 cup almond milk, 1 scoop protein powder.
Instructions: Blend until smooth.

Midnight Snack Nachos:
Ingredients: Tortilla chips, cheddar cheese, jalapenos, salsa, sour cream.
Instructions: Layer chips and cheese. Bake at 350F for 10 mins. Top with jalapenos, salsa, and sour cream.
